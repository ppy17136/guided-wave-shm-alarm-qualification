# P12-COPV reporting amendment A4: machine-readable evidence binding for the data-integrity gate

## Status

This is a post-outcome reporting and provenance amendment dated 15 August 2026. It does not amend the frozen scientific rule, score, support model, threshold, fusion rule, denominator, or outcome. The authoritative pre-outcome protocol remains `P12_COPV_confirmatory_preregistration_v1.md` (SHA-256 `a9240b3056d6de29658cf841137b33f9af77d626750aed5c5c687fa2fa3b66b4`).

## Reason for the amendment

Protocol Section 10, item 12 required that the data-integrity audit identify no unexplained state-specific channel or sampling-regime difference. The frozen execution serializer stored the adjudicated result under `no_unexplained_acquisition_asymmetry` as a Boolean. That representation preserved the outcome but did not expose the underlying metadata evidence in one machine-readable record.

## Evidence binding

The public reporting package therefore adds:

- `data/reports/p12_copv_a2_frequency_metadata_audit_v1.json`, which records equal representative sampling frequencies, burst programs, chirp/repetition indices, and raw-array shapes for baseline, irreversible, and reversible states;
- `data/reports/p12_copv_schema_audit_public_summary_v1.json`, a compact summary of the pre-existing LOCK1 official-JSON and representative-H5 schema audits; and
- `results/derived_tables/p12_acquisition_integrity_audit.json`, generated by `scripts/build_p12_acquisition_integrity_audit.py`.

The full official-JSON inventory covered 540 H5 files. Baseline and reversible states each had one full dataset-shape signature. The irreversible state had a second signature in 32 files because pressure and temperature metadata were absent: all 30 T37 records and two documented T55 random-ramp records. Core waveform, channel, frequency-index, sample, and sampling-frequency fields remained present. These gaps were documented by the official source and were routed to invalid/support abstention under the frozen rule. Sensor 20 was excluded symmetrically from all states before scoring. No unexplained state-specific channel or sampling-regime difference remained.

## Non-retroactivity

This evidence binding cannot turn a failed gate into a pass, rescue an unsupported condition, or change the strict P12 result. P12 remains FAIL (7 of 13 gates passed). The amendment only makes the evidence for the already pre-specified qualitative gate auditable and reproducible.
